What the model assumes

Widget’s standard cost card from the lecture: direct materials 2 lbs at $5.00 a pound and direct labor 0.5 hour at $20.00 an hour, per widget. The quantity allowed is output times the standard per widget. Materials: the price variance is (actual price − standard price) × pounds purchased, and the quantity variance is (pounds used − pounds allowed) × the standard price. Labor has no inventory, so hours are bought and used at the same moment: the rate variance is (actual rate − standard rate) × hours worked, and the efficiency variance is (hours worked − hours allowed) × the standard rate. A cost variance is U when the actual side exceeds the standard side and F otherwise. In the picture, the joint corner (extra price on the extra quantity) is drawn inside the price strip, because that is where the formula puts it: the buyer set the price on every unit bought, including the wasted ones.
