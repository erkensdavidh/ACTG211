The static budget is where the quarter was supposed to land. The first step is volume (the activity variance), the second is execution (the revenue and spending variances). The naive report skips the middle and reports only the total.

One variance per line, and every line compares a budget built for 40,000 cases with results from however many cases were sold.

The five-column report

The flexible budget is the plan’s formulas at the volume that actually happened. Execution lives between Actual and Flexible; volume lives between Flexible and Static.

What the model assumes

The static budget is the lecture’s: 40,000 cases at $20.00, variable costs of $12.40 a case, and fixed costs of $90,600 for the quarter. Actual revenue is cases sold times the actual price; actual variable costs are cases sold times the actual cost per case; actual fixed costs are whatever the lever says, at any volume. The flexible budget keeps the plan’s formulas and swaps in the actual volume. Labels follow the course rule: F when the line made income higher than the budget, U when lower; a fixed line shows $0 in the activity column. Connect and the textbook call the static budget the planning budget.
