What the model assumes

Phoenix: ROI = NOI ÷ average operating assets; residual income = NOI − minimum rate × average operating assets. The project’s NOI and assets add to the division’s. A manager graded on ROI takes the project only if the combined ROI is at least today’s; a manager graded on residual income takes it if the project’s own residual income is positive; the company wants it when the project’s ROI clears the minimum rate. Imperial: the order is 2,000 barrels. With idle capacity nothing is given up, so the opportunity cost is $0; with no idle capacity every transferred barrel replaces an outside sale, so the opportunity cost is the contribution margin per barrel lost (outside price − variable cost). Fixed costs do not change in either case. Everything is pre-tax, as in the course.
